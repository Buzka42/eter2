EterAstro.ttf
=============

A subset of two Google Noto faces, merged, containing only the 22 astrological
codepoints Eter draws:

  U+2609            Sun                  from Noto Sans Symbols 2
  U+263D..U+2647    Moon and planets     from Noto Sans Symbols
  U+2648..U+2653    the twelve signs     from Noto Sans Symbols

Both sources are licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1, which
permits subsetting, merging and redistribution with the software. The full
licence travels with the originals at:

  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssymbols
  https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssymbols2

Regenerate with tool/build_astro_font.py after changing the codepoint list.
The face is used for symbols only and never for text: Cormorant Garamond and
Inter remain the app's two voices.
